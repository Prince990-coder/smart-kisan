Income Support

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides income support to eligible landholding farmer families.

💰 Benefit ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments for eligible beneficiaries.
👨‍🌾 Important Eligibility and exclusions are determined according to the official PM-KISAN guidelines.

Crop insurance support for farmers against eligible crop-related risks.
